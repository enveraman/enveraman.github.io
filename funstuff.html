Food

There are some really nice places to eat in the Rutgers area (or, more generally, North/Central NJ). A full list of good places to come, but I've enjoyed...

  • Noodle Gourmet: it's walkable from Rutgers with inexpensive and pretty good Chinese food.
  • DunHuang Noodle: they have a really good $10 spicy beef noodle soup.
  • Leo's Barbecue: a nice Filipino lunch spot; their buko pandan, ube flan, and other desserts are really yummy.
